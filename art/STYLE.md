## Rules

- Outline 6px characters/props, 4px mid bg, 3px far.
- Seeded jitter 1–2px; flat fills; ≤1 shadow shape; sky gradient only.
- Robot cool against warm street; big heads on humans; readable at 25% zoom.
- Line boil + idle life applied in Godot, not baked into SVG.
